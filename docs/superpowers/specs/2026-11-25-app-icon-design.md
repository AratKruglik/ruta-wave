# App icon — design

Issue: [#28](https://github.com/AratKruglik/yt-music-mac/issues/28). Size: small change. Status: draft for approval, 2026-11-25.

## Intent

Rutawave needs its own icon for the Dock, Finder, the DMG window and notifications. The icon is a sound wave that is at the same time a rue flower (*Chervona Ruta*, see [PRD §1](../../00-prd.md#1-name-and-positioning)): red background, white graphics.

## Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

- Goal: one icon that reads as "sound + flower" at 1024 px and stays recognisable at 16 px.
- Goal: native look on macOS 26+ (Liquid Glass layers), flat fallback on macOS 14–15.
- Non-goal: the name and trademark check and the copy review from #28 — separate parts of the issue.
- Anti-goal: an icon that can be taken for YouTube Music. YouTube Music's mark is a white play triangle in a white circle on red, and this design is close to it (see Risk).

## Design (decided by the owner)

- **Direction C, "wave petals"**: three concentric outline rings, like sound ripples. Each one wobbles into petals.
- **The centre is a play shape that grows out of the waves**:
  - outer ring: 5 petals, pointing up;
  - middle ring: half 5 petals, half 3 lobes;
  - inner ring: 3 lobes pointing right, a rounded play shape;
  - centre: a filled white play triangle with rounded corners and slightly convex sides, optically centred (its centroid on the icon centre).
- **Colour**: a vertical "ruta" gradient, `#E8253F` at the top to `#9E0E24` at the bottom. Graphics are white. Ring opacity from outer to inner: 70 %, 85 %, 100 %. Not the flat `#FF0033` of YouTube Music.
- Reference geometry (1024 canvas with the icon shape on an 824 plate, centre 512; the generator scales it by 1024/824 because an `.icon` layer fills the whole shape): rings R = 300 / 225 / 150, amplitude 0.16 / 0.20 / 0.22, stroke 36 / 40 / 44 (thickened on 2026-11-25 for small sizes; 16 px @1x still blurs, accepted). Play: circumradius 66, side bulge 5 %, corner rounding about 30 px. Ring radius as a function of the angle φ: `r(φ) = R·(1 + a·((1−m)·cos 5(φ+π/2) + m·cos 3φ))`, with the 5→3 blend m = 0 / 0.5 / 1. The mockup is in `.superpowers/brainstorm/` and is not committed.

## Approaches

1. **Icon Composer `.icon` file** — layered (background, three rings, play), system Liquid Glass on macOS 26+, and Xcode 27 makes the flat fallback for macOS 14–15. **Chosen** — it matches the app's Liquid Glass UI.
2. Classic `AppIcon.appiconset` with PNGs rendered from one SVG — fully scripted, but flat on macOS 26+ and may be put on a grey plate there.
3. Both — duplicates what Xcode already does from the `.icon` file.

## Constraints

- macOS 14 deployment target; Xcode 27.2; the project is generated by XcodeGen 2.47 (`project.yml`). There is no asset catalog in `App/` yet.
- The icon layers are SVG, and a script generates them together with the `.icon` file's `icon.json`, so the geometry above can be reproduced. No manual Icon Composer step is needed: a spike on 2026-11-25 compiled a hand-written `.icon` with actool and built it through XcodeGen (`options.fileTypes.icon`). Icon Composer is still the place to fine-tune the glass.

## Risk (accepted by the owner)

A play triangle inside rings on a red background is close to the YouTube Music mark, especially at 16–32 px, where the inner ring looks almost round. #28 and PRD §1 forbid look-alikes. The owner chose this centre knowingly on 2026-11-25. The icon goes through the trademark review of #28 before release 0.1, and the "play-petal" variant (the same triangle without the 3-lobe inner ring) is the fallback if that review fails.
