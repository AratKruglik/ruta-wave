RutaWave

YouTube Music as a real Mac app.

A native SwiftUI interface, media keys, Now Playing and a menu bar player. No browser tab, no Electron.

Why RutaWave

I wanted YouTube Music to feel like a Mac app, so I built one. There is no official macOS app: a browser tab, a PWA or an Electron wrapper each runs a whole Chromium for one website and never feels like a Mac app.

Native

Every screen is SwiftUI: a real sidebar, menus, keyboard focus, VoiceOver and system window behaviour.

Light

No bundled browser. Playback runs in one hidden web view with the site's own player, driven from Swift.

Full catalogue

Your account, library, recommendations and Premium audio quality, because playback goes through the official web player.

No account needed

Guest mode lets you browse, search and play without signing in.

Everything you listen with

Listening

A player page with the Up next queue, lyrics and Related. Drag to reorder, Play next, radio chips, and audio that keeps playing on any Space.

Discovery

Home with mood chips, Explore with new releases, charts for any country, moods and genres, and search with suggestions and filters.

Your library

Playlists, songs, albums, artists, subscriptions and liked music. Create and edit playlists, and clean up your history.

Built for macOS

Now Playing in Control Centre, media keys and AirPods, keyboard shortcuts for everything, a menu bar item and a mini player.

Colours from the artwork

The window takes its tint from the cover of the album or playlist you are on.

Plugins

Track-change notifications and experimental karaoke lyrics that highlight each word as it is sung.

About the name

It comes from Chervona Ruta, a song every Ukrainian knows. “Ruta” stands for music and for red; “Wave” is sound.

What RutaWave does not do

Some of these are product decisions, some are honest limits.

  • It does not download tracks or keep audio for offline listening.
  • It does not block ads or analytics. Free accounts and guests hear the ads YouTube serves.
  • It is unofficial. Playback goes through the YouTube Music web player, so when Google changes the site, something can break until the next update.
  • It runs only on Macs with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later: no Intel, Windows, Linux or iOS, and no App Store.

Install

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later.

  1. Download

    Get the signed and notarized RutaWave DMG.

  2. Drag to Applications

    Open the DMG and drag RutaWave to the Applications folder.

  3. Listen

    It starts in guest mode. Sign in on Google's own page inside the app to get your library and Premium; your password never passes through RutaWave.

Updates itself

RutaWave checks for new versions and installs them with Sparkle. Pick the Stable or Beta channel in Settings → Updates.
