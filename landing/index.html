RutaWave

YouTube Music as a real Mac app.

A native SwiftUI interface, media keys, Now Playing and a menu bar player. No browser tab, no Electron.

YouTube Music has no Mac app.

So you end up with a browser tab, a PWA or an Electron wrapper. Each one runs a whole Chromium for a single website, the media keys go to whichever tab grabbed them last, and none of it behaves like a Mac app.

RutaWave is my attempt to fix that. Every screen is SwiftUI: a real sidebar, menus, keyboard focus, VoiceOver. Playback still goes through YouTube Music's own web player, hidden in a single web view, so your account, library, recommendations and Premium audio quality all work.

You don't even need an account. Guest mode browses, searches and plays without signing in.

What it looks like

Real screenshots of the current version. Scroll, or pick a screen.

  1. Home

    Mood chips across the top, then shelves with what you played lately and new releases. The app opens on whichever page you pick in Settings.

  2. Search

    ⌘F and a few letters. Suggestions appear as you type; then the top result, songs and filters for songs, videos, albums, artists and playlists.

  3. An artist

    Top songs, Shuffle, Radio and Subscribe, with More lists for every shelf.

  4. A playlist

    Play, shuffle, save to your library or share. Your own playlists live in the sidebar and can be edited right here.

  5. The player page

    ⌘P opens the artwork, the Up next queue, lyrics and Related. Drag tracks to reorder, add radio chips, save the queue as a playlist.

  6. Explore

    New releases, moods and genres, and daily and weekly charts for any country.

And what does not fit in a menu

System
Now Playing in Control Centre, media keys and AirPods controls. Audio keeps playing when the window is closed, hidden or on another Space.
Menu bar
An item with the current track; a click on it can play or pause. Hide the Dock icon, keep the window on top, launch at login.
Colour
The window takes its tint from the cover of the album or playlist you are on.
Queue
Up next with drag to reorder, Play next, Add to queue, radio chips and Save queue as playlist.
Lyrics
Lyrics on the player page, and an experimental karaoke plugin that highlights each word.
Links
Opens music.youtube.com links and rutawave:// links. Interface in English and Ukrainian.

About the name

It comes from Chervona Ruta, a song every Ukrainian knows. “Ruta” stands for music and for red; “Wave” is sound.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't download tracks or keep audio for offline listening.
  • It doesn't block ads or analytics. Free accounts and guests hear the ads YouTube serves.
  • It is unofficial. When Google changes the YouTube Music site, something can break until the next update.
  • It runs only on Macs with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. No Intel, Windows, Linux, iOS or App Store.

Install

  1. Download the DMG. It is signed and notarized.
  2. Open it and drag RutaWave to Applications.
  3. Launch it. It starts in guest mode; to get your library and Premium, sign in on Google's own page inside the app. Your password never passes through RutaWave.

After that it updates itself through Sparkle. Stable or Beta is a choice in Settings → Updates.
